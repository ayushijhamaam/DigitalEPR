Why not just an EPR portal

Customer, transporter, plant and compliance data live in one platform — no re-keying between spreadsheets, email threads and the CPCB portal.

Operational, not just regulatory

Runs pickup scheduling, weighbridge entry and recovery tracking — compliance reporting becomes a by-product of doing the work, not a separate exercise.
